The goal

Eat what you have.

So much food ends up in the bin because the date has passed, while it’s still perfectly fine. Zonde.

Datechecker explains the difference between “best before” and “use by”, and how long a product usually stays good after the date: closed or opened, in the fridge or the freezer. You get honest advice and what to look out for. And if it’s not at its best anymore? You get ideas to give it a second life.
