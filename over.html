About Zonde.

Our goal

Less needless waste, by using what’s already there.

In your closet, in your fridge, and soon in many more places. Zonde. makes free apps that help you do it, and brings together people who want the same thing.

The problem

We have enough. We just don’t use it.

Households around the world throw away 79 kilos of food per person every year, while much of it was still fine. Every second, a truckload of clothes is burnt or dumped. And we wear our clothes far less than we used to.

It’s not that people don’t care. You just forget what you have, or you’re not sure if something is still good. That’s exactly where simple, smart apps can help.

Why “Zonde.”?

Zonde is Dutch. It’s what you say when something good goes to waste: “Dat is zonde!”, “What a waste!” Your grandma says it when you leave half your dinner. That feeling is what this is all about. And the full stop at the end? That says it all.

Zonde. started in the Netherlands, which is why the apps are in Dutch for now. English versions are on their way.
